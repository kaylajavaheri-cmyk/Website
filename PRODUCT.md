# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Health brand founders, marketing leads, and brand managers at holistic/wellness companies. They're evaluating agencies and arrive via referral or discovery. They want to quickly assess credibility, taste, and the range of what Wyld West can do — not read a brochure. Secondary: holistic health creators who may seek talent representation.

## Product Purpose

Wyld West is a marketing agency for health-forward brands. This site is the agency's front door: an impression, a credential, and a quiet proof of taste. It should make a brand founder feel that Wyld West gets their world — without shouting about it.

Services offered:
- Talent Management (representing holistic health creators, e.g. @alexcbolivar, @holistichealingla, @the_detoxmama)
- Scaling and managing influencer/affiliate marketing programs
- In-house UGC content funnel: vetting creators, script writing, creative briefs
- Social media strategy
- Social media content and creative direction for photoshoots

Brand client portfolio: Plunge, Litesport, Akasha Superfoods, Kono Nutrition, Zuma Nutrition, Evie Ring, Chef Bae.

## Brand Personality

Earthy. Elevated. Uncompromising.

The western angle is not costume — it's a point of view. Think quiet confidence, land-and-craft sensibility, an agency that knows what it stands for and doesn't need to perform it loudly. Chic without being precious. Simple without being bland.

Entry experience: visitors arrive at a western card (like a hand-addressed calling card or a poker card bearing the WW mark) sealed with a wax closure. Clicking breaks the seal and opens the site. This sets the entire register: handmade, deliberate, memorable.

Logo: WW monogram with a lasso incorporated as a typographic element or enclosure — western craft without kitsch.

## Anti-references

The brief is to be unmistakably its own thing. Must avoid:
- **Generic agency sites**: "strategy. results. growth." with navy/grey palette and stock imagery. Zero soul.
- **Overtly trendy wellness aesthetic**: soft beige, leaf illustrations, Lora serif — the 2022–2024 wellness monoculture that has flooded the category.
- **Corporate health / pharma**: sterile blues, clinical whitespace, stock of doctors. Cold and wrong register.
- **Loud influencer-brand energy**: neon, heavy gradients, aggressive copy. Wyld West is an agency; it commands the room without yelling.

## Design Principles

1. **Quiet authority**: The site should feel like a well-made object, not a pitch deck. Let presence do the persuading.
2. **Western as sensibility, not costume**: The western references come through material texture, typography choice, and the entry ritual — not cowboy clip-art.
3. **Earned specificity**: Show real brands, real creators, real work. Concrete names over vague claims.
4. **Editorial restraint with committed color**: The earthy palette (deep sage green, warm bone, burnt orange as signal) is deployed with intent — not as wallpaper. Color has weight.
5. **One decisive gesture per section**: Each part of the page does one thing well. Hierarchy over density.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Strong contrast ratios for body text (≥4.5:1). Alt text as brand voice, not metadata. Reduced-motion alternative for all entry/entrance animations. Mobile-responsive from first build.
